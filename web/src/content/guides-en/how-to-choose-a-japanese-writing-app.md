Not every app that claims to teach you to write Japanese does the same thing. Some teach you to **recognize** characters and others make you **trace** them; among those that make you trace, only some check that you do it right. This guide gives you criteria to choose without relying on anyone's advertising, and complements [phone vs paper](/en/guides/practice-japanese-writing-phone-or-paper/).

## Seven criteria

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does it check the **order** of the strokes, not just the final shape? | An app that only compares the finished drawing lets you lock in the wrong order. |
| 2 | Does it check the **direction** of each stroke? | Direction is what separates pairs like シ and ツ (see [similar-looking kana](/en/guides/similar-looking-kana/)). |
| 3 | Does it correct you **as you write** or only at the end? | Correcting mid-stroke stops you repeating the mistake several times. |
| 4 | Can you practice **from memory**, without a guide? | Tracing is not the same as recalling. The full loop is in [how to learn hiragana by writing](/en/guides/how-to-learn-hiragana-by-writing/). |
| 5 | Does it start with **kana** and then move on to kanji by level? | The logical order is hiragana, katakana, then kanji (see [what a kanji is](/en/guides/what-is-a-kanji/)). |
| 6 | Can you **try for free** what you are about to pay for? | If the correction does not convince you, better to find out before paying. |
| 7 | How do you pay: one-time purchase, subscription or ads? | Compare the cost over a year, not the first month’s price. |

## The five-minute test

Before committing to an app, do this in its free mode:

1. Pick a kana with a clear order, for example し or ち.
2. Trace it **correctly** and see how the app responds.
3. Trace it **with the order changed** on purpose (start with the last stroke).
4. Trace it **in the opposite direction**.
5. Repeat with a multi-stroke kana, such as あ or ふ.

If the app accepts the stroke with the order or direction changed, it is teaching you to draw, not to write. If it catches it right away, it meets criteria 1 to 3.
