This week we reached a critical milestone: the deployment of **Phase 4**. We
have moved from the abstraction of CSV files to an interactive visual
environment. The Plaza is now a working reality.

We implemented a system so that pop-ups do not overlap between buildings such
as the Smithy or the Warehouse, and the inventory interface shows materials in
real time from a global dictionary.

Heroes now live in the Plaza and speak their first lines of dialogue, a
prelude to the *free will* system.
