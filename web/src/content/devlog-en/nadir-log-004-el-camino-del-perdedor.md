This week we focused on the pillar of progression: **the Portal and the
Barracks**. In Nadir nobody is special at the start. The management system is
designed so that you feel the weight of turning a basic unit into a 7-star
legend.

**The Portal.** We finished the summoning logic. It is not just chance: the
system injects rare elements such as Chaos or Blood that change the odds of the
starting attributes. Hero cards change colour with their rarity, up to the
Platinum finish reserved for 7-star units.

**The Barracks.** The biggest technical challenge of the week. A script reads
the hero files from the user's folder, sorts them by power and shows them in an
optimized grid. In addition, when assigning squads, the filters hide weapons
that another hero already has equipped.

**Memory.** The hero detail screen only loads the data it needs when opened, to
keep RAM usage low on phones.

Phases 1 and 2 consolidated. Next step: Phase 3, the Synthesis Lab (stat
inheritance).
